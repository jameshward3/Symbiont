## Recommended first offer

**Existing-Conditions Capture and Model Package**

- One local site visit in NY/NJ by the Founder.
- LiDAR/360/photo capture and documented coverage plan.
- Registered point cloud and photographic index.
- Existing-conditions Revit or AutoCAD model/drawings to an agreed level of accuracy and detail.
- Optional equipment/asset register and closeout/as-built update.
- Internal QA checklist and discrepancy log before delivery.

This package is deliberately narrow. Field capture can be performed locally by one person when site rules and scope permit. Point-cloud registration, modeling, drawing production, and independent QA can be subcontracted in full, while Symbiont retains client control, acceptance criteria, and final QA.

## Do not pursue first

- Large prime construction, architecture, engineering, or design-build obligations that require licenses, bonding, extensive past performance, or a staffed field organization.
- Expired DASNY solicitations, even when the official page still labels them “In Progress.” The due date controls.
- Opportunities behind registration, authentication, CAPTCHA, paywall, or terms gates until the Founder explicitly approves account creation or terms acceptance.
- Survey deliverables requiring a professional land surveyor unless a licensed surveyor owns and certifies that scope.
