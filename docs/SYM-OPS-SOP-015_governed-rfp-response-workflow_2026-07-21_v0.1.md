## Purpose

Control an opportunity from Pipeline handoff through bid/no-bid, RFP response development, authorized external issue, agreement, project delivery, QA/QC, and closeout. The workflow makes background-agent work visible at the decision point without turning every agent workspace into a primary navigation surface.

## Pipeline entry and handoff

1. Select a verified opportunity in **Pipeline**.
2. Review qualification, the ROM estimate, estimate confidence, bid signal, and missing information.
3. Select **Start governed response**.
4. The application creates or selects one live Action queue record. Demonstration records remain isolated behind Demo mode.
5. The handoff preserves the canonical source, scope summary, deadline, confidence, recommendation, and provenance.

The Pipeline ROM and the estimate in Actions are generated by the same model. This prevents a bid/no-bid number from diverging silently from the response price basis.

## Projects portfolio boundary

**Projects** begins at the Qualification gate and spans two controlled populations:

1. **Qualified pursuits** — non-demonstration Pipeline records at Qualification or later, including the shared ROM estimate, estimate confidence, bid recommendation, accountable owner, due date, next action, and whether the pursuit has entered Actions.
2. **Authorized delivery projects** — post-agreement project records from the governed delivery data plane.

A Lead does not appear in Projects until it reaches Qualification. A delivery record does not appear until contract and project-authorization controls create a live project. In **Live governed data** mode, the interface and delivery API return no demonstration project as a fallback when authorization is absent, the live table is empty, or the adapter is unavailable. Demo records appear only while Demo mode is explicitly active.

Selecting **Start governed response** from a qualified pursuit creates the same controlled Action record as the Pipeline handoff. If the handoff already exists, **Open in Actions** reopens it without creating a duplicate.

### Requirements matrix

The matrix records requirement ID, RFP section, obligation, formulated response, controlled knowledge sources, related work products, owner, and status. Initial rows derived from Pipeline evidence are starter records only. The full solicitation, attachments, exhibits, and amendments must be extracted before the matrix can be considered complete.

Knowledge matches are bounded. Sanitized historical capability aggregates can guide internal drafting, but AGT-005 must qualify file-level evidence before any external past-performance claim.

### Compiled draft

The compiled response preview includes the executive summary, requirement coverage, technical and management approach, deliverables, schedule, team, assumptions, exclusions, and pricing architecture. It is visibly marked **Draft — not for external issue**. The separate Draft editor supports Word and PDF export; exported files remain drafts until the release gate is complete.

### Estimate and bid decision

The ROM includes editable quantities, proposed rates, historical context, current market wage benchmarks, indirect assumptions, direct-cost allowances, contingency, source dates, confidence, and refresh due date.

Default formula:

`Proposed labor rate = market base wage × (1 + labor burden) × (1 + overhead) ÷ (1 − fee)`

Default assumptions are 35% labor burden, 25% overhead, 10% fee, and 10% estimate contingency. These are review assumptions, not approved company rates.

Current market references:

- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational wage benchmarks, May 2024
- GSA CALC+ pricing intelligence method and current ceiling-rate comparison step

Historical context references:

- Restricted 2025 invoice labor breakdown
- Restricted TIMPO proposal labor schedule dated 2000-11-20

Historical values are not escalated automatically. Finance must validate contract context, current costs, indirect rates, margin, location, wage-determination applicability, and vendor quotes.

## External-issue delivery gate

The **Approve response for external issue** action remains disabled until all required checks are satisfied:

1. Complete solicitation, attachments, and amendments are controlled.
2. Every RFP requirement is extracted, assigned, answered, and dispositioned.
3. Knowledge claims have file-level evidence and authorized review.
4. Technical solution, interfaces, risks, and acceptance criteria are reviewed.
5. Quantities, current market comparables, indirects, margin, and price are approved.
6. The compiled draft passes independent compliance and document QA/QC.
7. An authorized human approves the exact external-issue version.

The existing stage gate and named human approvals still apply. Completing the checklist does not bypass pricing approval, external-release approval, or gate confirmation.

## Current operating limitations

- Action records and edits are browser-session local until governed persistence is enabled.
- The system starts from the verified Pipeline scope summary; it does not yet ingest and parse every RFP attachment automatically.
- Direct-cost allowances remain provisional until current vendor quotes and quantities are linked.
- Market wage benchmarks are not the same as fully burdened contract prices.
- GSA ceiling-rate comparisons must be refreshed during price review and cannot be the sole determination of price reasonableness.
