# Sales-to-Delivery Handoff Checklist

- [ ] Approved proposal/SOW version identified
- [ ] Outcome, scope, exclusions, acceptance, dates, and commitments reconciled
- [ ] Stakeholders, access, data, dependencies, assumptions, risks, and decisions included
- [ ] Delivery reviewer, due date, and acceptance criterion assigned
- [ ] Commercial ambiguity owned by Sales until resolved
- [ ] Acceptance/rejection recorded as an event
