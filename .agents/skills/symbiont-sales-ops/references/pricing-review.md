# Pricing Review

Record currency, units, estimated labor by role, external cost, contingency, price, gross margin, confidence, exclusions, and source date. Validate arithmetic and compare against approved thresholds when available.

Never invent rates or thresholds. If policy is missing, report the proposed economics without declaring compliance. Create a decision request for discounts, pricing exceptions, low margin, material uncertainty, nonstandard terms, or unbounded scope. Do not expose internal cost or margin in client-facing drafts unless explicitly authorized.
