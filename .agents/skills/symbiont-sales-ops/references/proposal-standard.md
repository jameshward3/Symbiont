# Proposal and SOW Standard

Assemble a draft only after qualification and discovery evidence are linked. Include client outcome, context, scope, deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities, assumptions, dependencies, method, milestones, acceptance criteria, change control, data/security considerations, support, pricing basis, payment terms placeholder, validity placeholder, risks, and approvals.

Trace every deliverable to discovery evidence and an acceptance criterion. Label unresolved scope and all commercial/legal text as draft. Human approval is required before external use.
