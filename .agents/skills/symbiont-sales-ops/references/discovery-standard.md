# Discovery Standard

Prepare questions around desired business outcome, building/portfolio context, stakeholders and authority, current workflow/data/systems, pain and consequence, success measures, scope boundaries, constraints, timing, budget process, procurement, security/data rights, delivery acceptance, support, repeatability, and recurring operations.

Summaries must identify participants/date/source, verified current state, desired outcome, quantified evidence, decisions, assumptions, unknowns, risks, candidate scope, exclusions, next actions, and approvals. Do not convert an unverified statement into a requirement.
