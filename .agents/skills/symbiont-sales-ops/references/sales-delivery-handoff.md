# Sales-to-Delivery Handoff

Handoff only an approved commercial baseline. Package opportunity and client IDs, source proposal/SOW version, business outcome, scope/deliverables/exclusions, acceptance criteria, stakeholders, commitments, dates, assumptions, dependencies, data/access needs, risks, pricing basis, change control, communications, unresolved decisions, and evidence links.

Assign one delivery reviewer and due date. Status remains Review until delivery accepts, rejects with reasons, or requests clarification. Sales retains ownership of commercial ambiguity until resolved. Record acceptance as an append-only event; never overwrite the source proposal.
