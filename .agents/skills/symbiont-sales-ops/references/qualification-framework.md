# Qualification Framework

Score each factor 0–5 and record evidence. Weighting: strategic fit 20%, client need 15%, buyer/access 15%, value and budget 15%, delivery feasibility 15%, timing 10%, repeatability/recurring potential 10%. The score is the weighted result on a 100-point scale.

- **Pursue:** 70–100, no critical gate fails, next action dated.
- **Nurture:** 45–69 or one remediable gate is unknown.
- **Decline:** below 45, prohibited/risky work, no credible need, or delivery economics cannot meet policy.

Critical gates: authorized buyer path, legitimate problem, feasible outcome, acceptable data/safety/legal posture, delivery capacity, and pricing review before commitment. Missing evidence is not a positive score. Present factor scores, sources, confidence, gate failures, recommendation, next action, owner, date, and approvals.
