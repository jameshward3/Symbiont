# Incident severity

- SEV-1 Critical: safety, security, privacy, legal, financial, contractual, destructive, major client impact, unauthorized external action, or significant data loss/corruption. Stop or contain, preserve evidence, escalate immediately.
- SEV-2 High: material operational/client impact, widespread or repeated failure, or critical milestone threat. Contain and escalate immediately.
- SEV-3 Medium: limited failure, workaround, delayed internal processing, or degraded performance. Assign owner and recovery target.
- SEV-4 Low: minor defect, isolated warning, or non-urgent improvement. Plan remediation.

Reclassification must preserve the original assessment, new evidence, actor, authority, timestamp, rationale, and approver. Never downgrade to suppress escalation.
