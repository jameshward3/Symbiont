# Quality planning

Record project, client, deliverable, contract/authorization, scope, exclusions, approved acceptance criteria, standards, source-data versions, formats, classification, author, independent reviewer, approver, deadline, review depth, and checklist version.

Define the review objective, scope, automated and manual checks, sampling method, critical interfaces, risk-critical elements, schedule, evidence requirements, and release criteria. Review 100% of safety-, cost-, interface-, contractual-, security-, and client-critical items. Use risk-based sampling only for lower-risk repetitive content and record the method.

Require author self-checks for completeness, client/project identity, scope traceability, calculations, units, coordinates, levels/grids, naming, links, references, lineage, metadata, clarity, usability, accessibility, integrity, format, permissions, and contract requirements.

Do not begin independent QC on a mutable candidate. Record an immutable version and content hash. If the hash changes, stop, register a revision, and identify every check requiring repetition.
