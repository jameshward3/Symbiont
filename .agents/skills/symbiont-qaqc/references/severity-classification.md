# Severity classification

- `Critical`: unsafe reliance, major contractual failure, unusable/corrupt delivery, security exposure, significant client harm, or materially wrong operational decision. Block issue and escalate immediately.
- `Major`: material scope, acceptance, technical, coordination, integrity, usability, or client-requirement failure. Block issue until independently verified.
- `Minor`: limited standards failure or quality reduction that does not materially prevent use. Correct before issue unless an authorized exception is recorded.
- `Suggestion`: optional improvement, not required for compliance or acceptance. Does not block issue.

Base severity on credible impact and governing requirements. Record uncertainty; never lower severity to preserve schedule. Critical and Major findings require independent verification and cannot be closed by the author.
