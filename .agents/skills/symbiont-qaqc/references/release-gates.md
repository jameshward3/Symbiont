# Release gates

Verify exact client, project, deliverable set, candidate version/hash, scope completeness, criteria satisfaction, current checklist completion, retained evidence, required approvals, names/metadata, accessible sources, link integrity, permissions, security checks, issued format, approved claims, transmittal reconciliation, exclusion of working files, and confidentiality.

Apply these outcomes:

- `Blocked`: baseline incomplete/conflicting, candidate unlocked or modified, obsolete checklist, security or contractual uncertainty, or missing authoritative evidence.
- `Rework required`: any unresolved Critical or Major finding.
- `Conditionally ready`: no Critical/Major blocker, but unresolved Minor findings, incomplete noncritical checks, or pending authorized disposition remain.
- `Ready for approval`: all mandatory checks pass, all findings are resolved or authorized, evidence is retained, and approvals are recorded.

This is a recommendation only. The authorized Project Lead or human approver decides client issue.
