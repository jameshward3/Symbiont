# Project Health and Exceptions

Calculate from current, cited evidence. Suppress or label a metric when inputs are missing or stale.

- **Green:** schedule variance <=5%, margin variance <=3 points, and no overdue critical item or other intervention trigger.
- **Amber:** schedule variance >5% through 10%; margin variance >3 through 7 points; a critical issue; missing required information; blocked dependency; failed quality review; overdue client input/approval; unclear or expanding scope; or intervention needed to protect an outcome.
- **Red:** schedule variance >10%; margin variance >7 points; missed/likely missed critical milestone; material client, contractual, financial, quality, or delivery outcome at risk; safety/legal/data incident; or unauthorized commitment.

Use the most severe supported condition as overall health. Variances use absolute magnitude; describe favorable/unfavorable direction separately. Schedule variance percentage is forecast delay divided by baseline duration × 100 when those inputs are valid.

An action is overdue when its due date has ended in the agreed project timezone and status is not Complete, Closed, or Cancelled.

Each exception includes evidence, impact, accountable owner, corrective action, due date, escalation path, and validation criteria. Escalate Red immediately. Escalate >10% schedule or >7-point margin variance within four business hours. Never accept material risk; route acceptance to the authorized human and AGT-001.
