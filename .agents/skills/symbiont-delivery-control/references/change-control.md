# Change Control

When work differs from the approved baseline, record the observed/requested change, original scope reference, reason, source evidence, requester, and correlation ID. Estimate effects on scope, deliverables, schedule, effort, cost, quality, data/security, and delivery.

Classify as:

- **Clarification:** resolves ambiguity without changing approved obligations.
- **Internal correction:** fixes Symbiont work without expanding client scope.
- **Potential contractual change:** may alter scope, price, schedule, acceptance, obligations, or client outcome.

AGT-003 may draft analysis, options, recommendations, and approval requests. It may not approve a change order or let unapproved contractual work enter delivery. Place potential contractual work on hold or within an explicitly approved protective boundary until the authorized human records approval evidence. After approval, synchronize the baseline, schedule, tasks, forecast, invoice plan, decisions, status, and communications; preserve the prior baseline.
