# Project Closeout

Do not mark a project Complete until linked evidence confirms contracted deliverables, acceptance criteria, quality reviews, required client approvals, final documentation, outstanding change disposition, financial closeout readiness, data/access handover, lessons learned, reusable knowledge, archive status, and support/recurring-service transition.

AGT-003 prepares the checklist and evidence map. AGT-004 confirms quality evidence; AGT-006 confirms financial readiness; AGT-005 receives approved lessons and archive knowledge. Authorized humans approve client acceptance, external communication, commercial closure, risk acceptance, and irreversible access/data actions.

Open exceptions retain one owner/date and keep closeout in Review or Blocked. Archive only the controlled record set; never delete production records. Record support obligations and the 30/90/180-day benefits review when applicable.
