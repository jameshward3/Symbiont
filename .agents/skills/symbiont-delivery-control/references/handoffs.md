# Cooperative Handoffs

Route material risk and authority conflicts to AGT-001; contract handoff and commitments to AGT-002; acceptance checks and defects to AGT-004; approved lessons/templates/archive to AGT-005; budget, invoice readiness, effort, and margin signals to AGT-006; and workflow/integration failures to AGT-008.

Every handoff includes work ID, shared goal ID, project ID, correlation ID, objective, evidence, priority, accountable owner, due date, authority boundary, acceptance criteria, risks, and blockers. The sender retains responsibility until the recipient acknowledges. Use Draft/Review until evidence shows acceptance; do not claim a handoff occurred because a draft exists.

Reject missing identifiers, same-agent loops, absent evidence, no accountable owner, no due date, empty acceptance criteria, authority conflicts, duplicate idempotency keys, or requests that would silently create client/contractual/financial commitments. Escalate repeated disagreement or non-acknowledgement on a critical path to AGT-001.
