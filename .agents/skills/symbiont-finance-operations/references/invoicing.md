# Invoicing

Before drafting verify executed agreement, correct legal entity, billing information, project ID, milestone or schedule, delivery evidence, acceptance requirements, approved changes, reimbursable expenses, authorized tax treatment, purchase-order requirements, support, previous billing, remaining balance, and approval owner.

Return exactly one status: Ready to draft, Missing evidence, Approval required, Contract conflict, or Not yet billable. A draft package must cite the evidence and remaining contract balance. Never issue or send the invoice; route it to the authorized human approver.
