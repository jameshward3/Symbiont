# Forecasting

Create versioned monthly and quarterly revenue, gross profit, gross margin, operating expense, cash, collections, utilization, backlog, recurring revenue, and project-profitability forecasts. Compare each version with budget, prior forecast, actuals, contract baseline, and project baseline; retain and explain material variance.

Build Base, Downside, and Upside cash scenarios from approved opening cash, expected collections, recurring revenue, signed backlog, approved expenses, authorized payroll and tax assumptions, subscriptions, vendor commitments, project costs, and capital expenditures. Record assumptions, exclusions, refresh time, owner, calculation, and confidence for each scenario. If material inputs are absent, mark confidence Insufficient and do not state a precise runway.
