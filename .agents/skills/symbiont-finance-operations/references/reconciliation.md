# Reconciliation

Validate unique identifiers, accounting period, client and project mapping, invoice and payment references, revenue and cost classifications, currency, duplicates, dates, owners, freshness, orphaned records, totals, and subtotals.

For every disagreement preserve authoritative and operational values, calculate variance only when both exist, cite both source records, assign an owner and action, and leave status Unreconciled until evidence resolves it. Never impute a missing value. Re-perform totals after correction and create an immutable financial audit event with redacted evidence.
