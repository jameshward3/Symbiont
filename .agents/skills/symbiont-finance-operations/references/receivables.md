# Receivables

Track invoice, client, project, issue and due dates, amount, receipts, balance, days outstanding, dispute, collection owner, next action, promised payment date, and evidence. Flag approaching due, overdue, older than 30 days, older than 45 days, disputes, missing owner, missing action, and payment mismatch. Escalate items above 45 days to AGT-001. Draft reminders only for human approval; never contact clients autonomously.
