# Finance handoffs

Every handoff includes work ID, shared goal ID, correlation ID, source, period, owner, authority, assumptions, confidence, required action, deadline, and acceptance evidence.

- AGT-001: financial health, Red margin, cash risk, aged receivables, stale or missing forecasts, concentration, authority decisions.
- AGT-002: approved pipeline probabilities, pricing inputs, commercial forecast, contract status.
- AGT-003: progress, actual labor, remaining effort, changes, milestone and acceptance evidence.
- AGT-004: rework and quality-cost evidence.
- AGT-005: controlled definitions and procedures.
- AGT-008: finance-automation health, reconciliation failures, and recovery evidence.
- AGT-010: product economics and recurring-revenue assumptions.
- AGT-013: retention, renewal, and recurring-revenue signals.

Immediately escalate suspected fraud, unauthorized activity, exposed credentials, material inconsistency, billing error, cash risk, data loss, or contractual/tax uncertainty requiring professional review.
