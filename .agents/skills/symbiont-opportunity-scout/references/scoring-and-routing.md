# Scoring and Routing

Score each component on 0–100 using canonical evidence, then calculate the weighted total:

| Component | Weight | High-score evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Service fit | 25% | Reality capture, BIM/VDC, digital twin, asset information, IoT/building data, facilities technology, or energy management is explicit |
| Buyer fit | 15% | Asset-intensive owner/operator with portfolio, campus, or repeatable delivery potential |
| Timing | 15% | Actionable deadline with at least ten business days of lead time |
| Value | 10% | Stated value or credible scope supports a commercially meaningful engagement |
| Evidence | 15% | Official canonical record is complete, current, and internally consistent |
| Access | 10% | Public access, usable contact/path, and no unresolved registration barrier |
| Recurrence | 10% | Program, portfolio, framework, multi-site, or operational follow-on potential |

Round the weighted total to the nearest integer. Confidence measures evidence reliability, not desirability. Freshness is based on the latest verified observation.

- **70–100:** Draft a Handoff to AGT-002 with status `Review`; AGT-002 must explicitly accept it.
- **50–69:** Add to the watchlist with a dated monitoring trigger.
- **0–49:** Archive with a specific reason.

Do not override thresholds for enthusiasm. Escalate priority conflicts to AGT-001. A critical legal, access, privacy, or authority risk can force watchlist/archive regardless of score; explain the gate.
