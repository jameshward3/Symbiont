# Quality and retrieval

Score ownership 20%, freshness 20%, completeness 20%, findability 15%, usage 15%, and link health 10%. Mark unavailable dimensions `Unknown`; compute a normalized score only from known evidence and disclose missing dimensions. Below 80 enters remediation. Below 60 is excluded from general agent guidance but remains visible to authorized reviewers.

Review policies and SOPs annually, technical standards and templates every six months, agent charters and automations quarterly and after material model or tool changes, and project records at milestones and closeout unless an approved policy says otherwise.

Search metadata, tags, synonyms, relationships, status, area, source, owner, project, client, opportunity, goal, and agent. Rank authorized Active authoritative records first. Show status, version, source, freshness, owner, quality, and warnings. Archived and superseded records may appear only when history is explicitly requested and must never be current guidance.
