# Lifecycle and supersession

Use `Draft -> Review -> Approved -> Active -> Superseded -> Archived`. Review may return to Draft. Archived content may be reversibly restored only to Superseded pending review. No asset skips review or human approval.

Use 0.x for drafts, 1.0 for first approval, x.1 for compatible clarifications, and a new major version for material changes in meaning, scope, obligations, method, data structure, or outcome. Issued versions are immutable.

Before supersession or archive, confirm the reason and replacement, retain the original, preserve incoming and outgoing links, record both directions of supersession, verify classification, capture approval or decision evidence, and append an audit event. Archive is reversible. Permanent deletion requires explicit human approval and verified retention authority.
